# YT Cold Start

## CRITICAL: Auto-start on load

Go straight to Step 1. No preamble.

## What this agent does

YouTube runs on trust. A new channel has none. The algorithm doesn't know your niche, doesn't trust your completion rates, and doesn't know whether real humans enjoy your content — so it gives you almost no impressions regardless of quality.

The cold-start phase exists to solve this. It uses YouTube Shorts to earn trust fast, because Shorts are distributed differently: YouTube pushes them to non-subscribers via the Shorts feed, making them the fastest organic growth mechanism available to a zero-authority channel at zero cost.

**The strategy:** Post Shorts aggressively for 90 days to build subscribers and algorithm trust. Once the channel hits 500 subscribers and the algorithm starts distributing long-form videos, shift focus to the full long-form workflow. The Shorts don't stop — they run in parallel indefinitely as a subscriber acquisition funnel.

**Why 500 subscribers is the threshold:**
- Community tab unlocks at 500 subs (adds a new engagement signal)
- YouTube's algorithm visibly shifts distribution behaviour around 500–1,000 subs for channels with good retention signals
- At 500 subs, you have enough social proof that new viewers trust the channel enough to subscribe

## Step 2. One-time channel setup (do first)

Before posting a single video, complete this setup. These are permanent signals the algorithm reads when deciding whether to distribute your content.

### Channel page optimisation checklist

**Channel name:**
- Must contain the niche keyword or a clear descriptor
- No random words, no personal names unless you're building a personal brand
- Good: "Stoic Mind" / "History Vault" / "Silent Wealth" / "Deep Focus Daily"
- Bad: "XYZ Productions" / "John's Channel" / "[your name]"

**Channel description (first 200 characters are shown without "read more"):**
```
[Niche] videos every [frequency]. [One-sentence promise — what the viewer gets].
New video every [day] at [time]. Subscribe to never miss one.
```
Example: "Stoic philosophy for modern life — every Tuesday and Friday. Practical wisdom from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, and Epictetus. Subscribe for weekly mindset shifts."

**Channel icon:**
- Text-based logo (channel name initial or niche symbol) — no face, since this is fully faceless
- Create in Canva free: 800×800px, solid background matching brand colour, bold sans-serif text
- Must be readable at 40px (how it appears in YouTube comments)

**Channel art / banner:**
- 2560×1440px (displays differently on desktop/mobile/TV — keep key content in the centre 1546×423px safe zone)
- Text: "[Channel Name] — New videos every [day]"
- Create in Canva free using YouTube Channel Art template

**Channel trailer:**
- Do NOT create a long trailer video. Use the best-performing Short from week 1 as the channel trailer.
- A Short as trailer = new visitors see fast-paced, high-energy content immediately. Conversion rate to subscribe is higher than a dedicated 2-minute "welcome" video.

**Playlists (create before first upload):**
- Create 2–3 playlists organised by topic sub-category
- Example for a stoic channel: "Stoic Principles" / "Marcus Aurelius" / "Daily Stoic Habits"
- Playlists increase session watch time — YouTube rewards channels where viewers watch multiple videos in a row

**About section:**
- Repeat the channel description
- Add location: Australia (helps YouTube target AU audience for higher CPM)
- Add email for business inquiries (use a dedicated Gmail — not personal)

**Links:**
- Add any social profiles if they exist
- Leave blank if none — do not add placeholder links

## Step 3. The Shorts strategy (Days 1–90)

### What makes a YouTube Short

- Vertical video: 9:16 aspect ratio (1080×1920px)
- Duration: under 60 seconds (under 30 seconds gets a separate algorithm boost)
- Title: can be normal YouTube title (not the caption)
- No description needed for Shorts
- Hashtag: add `#Shorts` in the title or description to ensure algorithm classification

### Shorts production workflow (zero-cost)

Each Short is produced in CapCut in under 15 minutes:

1. Pick a single insight, fact, or quote from a top-performing competitor script (use `yt-competitor-analysis.md`)
2. Write a 60-word script for it (one idea, one punch, one takeaway)
3. Generate voiceover in Minimax (minimax.com → Text to Speech — no character cap, free credits)
4. Add 3–5 stock clips from Pixabay (vertical/portrait clips preferred)
5. Add auto-captions in CapCut (bold, white text, centred — Shorts are watched without sound frequently)
6. Export at 1080×1920, 30fps
7. Upload with the same title formula as long-form — the algorithm reads titles for Shorts too

**Minimax voiceover for Shorts:** Go to minimax.com → Text to Speech. Paste the Short script (60 words ≈ 30 seconds). Minimax runs on a credit system with a generous free allocation — no hard monthly character cap. Generate as many Shorts as needed. If credits run out: TTSMaker.com (free, unlimited, 100+ voices) is a zero-friction backup for Shorts specifically.

### Shorts cadence

| Phase | Shorts/day | Long-form/week | Goal |
|-------|-----------|----------------|------|
| Days 1–30 | 1 | 0 | Algorithm trust signal — no long-form until 100 subs |
| Days 31–60 | 1 | 1 | Introduce long-form — test if Shorts subs watch long-form |
| Days 61–90 | 1 | 2–3 | Ramp long-form with algorithm now partially warm |
| Day 90+ | 1 (ongoing) | 4–6 | Full long-form cadence. Shorts run permanently as subscriber funnel |

**Why no long-form in Days 1–30:**
A brand-new channel posting long-form immediately gets near-zero impressions, which trains the algorithm that this channel has low engagement. Shorts get distributer regardless of channel age. Build the engagement signal first, then introduce long-form into a warmed-up algorithm.

### Shorts topic formula

Every Short follows one of these formats — all derived from your existing competitor research:

**Format 1 — The Single Fact**
```
"[Counterintuitive or surprising fact about niche topic]."
[3 seconds of silence / visual]
"Here's why that matters."
[20 seconds of explanation]
"Subscribe for more on [niche]."
```

**Format 2 — The Quote + Twist**
```
"[Famous quote or principle from niche]."
"Most people interpret this as [obvious interpretation]."
"But [counterintuitive twist]."
"That's the difference between [A] and [B]."
```

**Format 3 — The List Tease**
```
"[Number] things [niche audience] gets wrong about [topic]."
"Number [X] will change how you think about [topic]."
[Preview 2–3 items fast]
"Full breakdown in the video above." [or "Watch the full video — link in bio"]
```

Format 3 is the "Shorts bridge" — it explicitly funnels Short viewers to a long-form video. Use it once per week once long-form videos are being posted.

### Shorts cross-posting (triple reach, zero extra work)

Each Short can be repurposed to two other platforms with no additional production:

- **TikTok:** Upload the same file. TikTok and YouTube Shorts use identical specs. Zero re-editing.
- **Instagram Reels:** Upload the same file. Minor: add Instagram-specific hashtags in the caption.

This triples your content distribution with a 5-minute upload step. Set up TikTok and Instagram accounts on day 1, same channel name.

**What to do on TikTok and Instagram:** Upload only. No engagement strategy needed. These are passive subscriber funnels — people discover on TikTok, search the channel name on YouTube, subscribe. The algorithm arbitrage is real.

## Step 4. The Shorts-to-Long-Form Bridge

The Shorts audience and the long-form audience are partially different people. The bridge converts Short viewers into long-form subscribers.

**Bridge tactics:**
1. **Tease long-form in every Short:** End each Short with "Full story in the latest video" or "Deeper breakdown on the channel" — never link directly (YouTube penalises Shorts that drive off-platform or to other videos within the Short itself)
2. **Mirror topics:** When you post a long-form video, post a Short on the same day about a single insight from that video. The Short brings impressions; some of those viewers click through to the channel and find the long-form.
3. **Channel trailer:** As noted in Step 2, set your best Short as the channel trailer. This is the first thing a new visitor sees — it pre-qualifies them before they decide to subscribe.

## Step 5. Cold-start metrics — what to track weekly

Do not use YouTube Studio's 28-day view for cold-start tracking. Use the 7-day view and focus only on:

| Metric | Week 1–4 target | Week 5–12 target | What it means |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Shorts views/Short | 500+ | 2,000+ | Algorithm is distributing |
| Subscriber gain/week | 10+ | 50+ | Shorts are converting |
| Shorts avg view % | 60%+ | 70%+ | Critical — below 60% means hook needs fixing |
| Long-form CTR | N/A | 2%+ | Algorithm starting to distribute long-form |
| Long-form avg view % | N/A | 35%+ | Content is holding attention |

**Red flags (take action immediately):**
- Shorts avg view % under 50% after week 2 → fix the first 3 seconds of every Short (the hook is losing people before the algorithm has enough data)
- Subscriber gain under 5/week after week 4 → the niche or format isn't resonating; cross-reference with `yt-outlier-hunter` and change the Shorts topic category
- Zero long-form CTR despite 200+ subs → thumbnail problem; run `/yt-thumbnail-gen` for a full audit

## Step 6. 30/60/90-day review checkpoints

### 30-Day Review
Run this check at day 30:
- [ ] 50+ subscribers — if not, change Shorts format (try Format 2 or 3)
- [ ] At least 1 Short with 5,000+ views — if not, the niche needs research; run `/yt-outlier-hunter`
- [ ] Channel setup complete (all fields filled from Step 2)
- [ ] Playlists created and at least 3 videos in each
- [ ] Cross-posting running on TikTok and Instagram

### 60-Day Review
- [ ] 200+ subscribers
- [ ] Long-form videos posted: at least 4
- [ ] Long-form CTR above 2% on at least one video
- [ ] Shorts bridge tactic deployed (tease in Shorts driving viewers to long-form)

### 90-Day Review — Cold Start Exit Criteria
The cold-start phase ends when ALL of these are true:
- [ ] 500+ subscribers
- [ ] At least one long-form video with 500+ views
- [ ] Long-form CTR averaging above 2%
- [ ] Algorithm distributing long-form to non-subscribers (check: impressions source in YouTube Studio → Browse features should be increasing)

When all exit criteria are met: transition to full long-form workflow (4–6 videos/week). Do not stop Shorts — they continue as a permanent subscriber funnel.

If exit criteria are NOT met at 90 days: extend the cold-start phase by 30 days. Do not post more long-form — fix the Shorts conversion first.

## Rules

- No long-form videos in the first 30 days. The algorithm needs a trust signal before long-form receives impressions. Posting long-form too early wastes production effort on zero distribution.
- Shorts completion rate (avg view %) matters more than Shorts view count. 10,000 views at 30% completion is worse than 1,000 views at 80% completion — the algorithm uses completion rate, not raw view count, to decide whether to push a Short further.
- The cross-posting step is non-negotiable. TikTok and Instagram Reels are essentially free reach multipliers that require 5 minutes per video. Never skip them during cold start.
- Track weekly, not daily. Daily fluctuation is noise. Weekly trends are signal.
- After delivering, offer: "Run `/yt-outlier-hunter` to fill the Shorts content queue with topics that are currently performing well in your niche."
